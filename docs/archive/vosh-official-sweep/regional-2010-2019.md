## Added cards

- `vosh-2010-11-regional-warehouses-cement-routing`: 11.4, R. Karasev. Direct connected-graph transport problem; answer `2010 = n-1`.
- `vosh-2014-15-regional-grid-rainbow-rectangle`: 10.8, D. Khramtsov. Grid coloring becomes an edge-coloring of `K_{n,n}`; threshold `k=2n`.
- `vosh-2017-18-regional-friendship-triangle-factor`: 11.9, S. Berlov and N. Vlasova. Friendship graph with a triangle factor after deleting any vertex; minimum `198`.

## Already present or skipped

- 2013/14 regional 10-11 problem 8 on even rows and columns is already present as `vosh-2013-14-regional-even-rows-columns`.
- 2011/12 regional 11.6 is tournament-like but the official formulation is primarily a round-robin result-count problem; skipped in this small confident batch to avoid a borderline import.
- 2015/16 regional 11.7 has a graph-style visualization in the official comments, but the core solution is not essentially graph-theoretic; skipped.
- 2019/20 regional day 2 contains "график" of a polynomial, not graph theory; skipped.

## Risks and follow-up

- The sweep used extracted PDF text plus manual reading of the official solution passages. OCR/text extraction occasionally mangles symbols, so formulas in the added cards were normalized by mathematical reading rather than copied mechanically.
- I did not add weak graph-in-solution candidates. A later high-reasoning pass could inspect 2011/12 11.6 and other combinatorics problems for possible graph formulations.
- `problems.ru` was not needed for these three cards because official PDFs already contained statement, solution and author/proposer data.
