# Open Olympiad of FML 239: source survey

Scope: Санкт-Петербургская открытая олимпиада ФМЛ/школы 239 по математике.

Date: 2026-05-03.

## Official / near-official sources found

- Official 239.ru Mathematical Center page confirms that the center runs the Open Olympiad of FML 239 and the fifth-grade open olympiad. Source: https://239.ru/math-center
- Official 239.ru news post for 2021 confirms the revived Open Olympiad, says participants get 8 problems in 5 hours, and links to conditions and results. Source: https://239.ru/math-center/tpost/zbh6j2ddu1-otkritaya-olimpiada
- Official 239.ru news post for 2024 confirms the 31 March 2024 Open Olympiad of Lyceum 239, two divisions 8-9 and 10-11, and links to the problem PDF and results. Source: https://239.ru/tpost/i9zz8rjt31-proshla-otkritaya-olimpiada-239-litseya
- Annual published books "Задачи Санкт-Петербургской олимпиады школьников по математике" include a section "Олимпиада 239 школы" with statements and solutions. Example: the 2015 book description says it includes the Open Olympiad of FML 239 and that all problems have detailed solutions. Source: https://publications.hse.ru/pubs/share/direct/227211409.pdf
- Example 2017 annual book PDF has a table of contents section "Олимпиада 239 школы" and later "Решения задач"; extracted text shows the 239 section starts on page 23. Source: https://www.viu-online.ru/science/bulletin/2011/Kohas_K._Berlov_S._Hrabrov_A._i_dr._-_Zadachi_Sankt-Peterburgskoj_olimpiady_shkolnikov_po_matematike_2017_goda_-_2018.pdf

## Archive status

- I did not find a single official public "all years" archive page for the Open Olympiad of FML 239 on 239.ru.
- Official 239.ru has individual news posts with problem links for at least 2021 and 2024.
- Published annual SPbMO books are the best structured source for older years when available: they include the 239 Olympiad section and solutions, but they are annual PDFs/books rather than a standalone 239 archive.
- Search results indicate annual books for at least 2000, 2015, and 2017 include "Открытая олимпиада ФМЛ №239"/"Олимпиада 239 школы"; this needs systematic year-by-year collection.

## Initial graph candidates from downloaded official statements

- 2021, 8-9, problem 3: explicit non-bipartite graph on 239 vertices, minimum degree at least 3, shortest odd cycle bound.
- 2021, 10-11, problem 8: friendship graph with parity condition on common friends and postcard choices.
- 2024, 8-9, problem 2: noncrossing segments on 2n points, asks for n disjoint segments; geometric matching candidate.
- 2024, 10-11, problem 8: pair 2n points and cover pairs by disks avoiding other points; geometric matching candidate.

## Manual-check notes

- The 2021 and 2024 PDFs found via 239.ru appear to contain conditions only; no official solutions were in those downloaded files.
- For solution-confirmed cards, prefer annual SPbMO book PDFs when they include the relevant year's 239 section and solutions.
- For condition-only inclusion, the official 239.ru-linked PDFs are acceptable statement sources.
